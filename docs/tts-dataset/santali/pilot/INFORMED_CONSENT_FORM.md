# Bhasha Setu — Santali Speech Dataset Participant Informed Consent Form
## Plain-Language Voice Contributor Agreement

**Form Version**: `v1.0.0`  
**Applicable Dataset**: `Santali Speech Pilot (pilot-v0.1.0)`  
**Language of Record**: Santali / English Bilingual Agreement

---

### 1. What is this project?
Bhasha Setu is an open-source tribal language bridge project developed to support migrant teachers, healthcare workers, and community members in communicating effectively in Santali and other indigenous languages.

---

### 2. What are we asking you to do?
You are invited to contribute your voice by reading selected short sentences written in the **Ol Chiki script** in a quiet recording studio booth. 
* A recording session lasts between **20 and 45 minutes**.
* You will read approximately 15 to 35 short sentences.
* You may re-record any sentence if you make a mistake or feel uncomfortable.
* You are encouraged to take rest breaks at any time.

---

### 3. What will be recorded?
* Only your spoken voice reading the approved prompt sentences.
* We will **NOT** record your personal private conversations, phone calls, or unscripted comments.

---

### 4. How will your voice recording be used?
* **Educational & Linguistic Research**: To study Santali acoustic phonetics, prosody, and pronunciation naturalness.
* **Speech Model Training (Optional)**: If you grant permission, your recorded audio may be used to train or fine-tune an open-source synthetic Text-to-Speech (TTS) voice model for Santali.
* **Zero PII Exposure**: Your voice recordings will be identified only by a pseudonymous number (for example, `SAT-SPK-0001`). Your real name, phone number, address, and identity documents will **never** be included in the speech dataset or shared with model trainers.

---

### 5. Specific Permission Options
You have the full right to choose which permissions you wish to grant:

| Permission Area | Description | Grant Permission? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Model Training** | Use my voice to train open-source Santali speech synthesis models | `[YES] / [NO]` |
| **Non-Commercial Research** | Share my voice data with university linguistic researchers | `[YES] / [NO]` |
| **Commercial Application** | Permit commercial entities to use speech models built from this data | `[YES] / [NO]` |
| **Public Redistribution** | Include my audio in an openly downloadable public research dataset | `[YES] / [NO]` |

*(Note: If you select `[NO]` for Model Training, your audio will be used exclusively for linguistic auditing and will never be fed into neural model training).*

---

### 6. Can I change my mind and withdraw my voice later?
**Yes, absolutely.** You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time without giving any reason:
* If you withdraw, we will immediately mark your consent record as `WITHDRAWN`.
* All your audio recordings and transcripts will be permanently removed from our active dataset manifests and excluded from future model training releases.
* Models that were already compiled prior to withdrawal cannot always be un-trained retroactively, but no new versions will ever include your voice.

---

### 7. Who can I contact if I have questions or want to withdraw?
To exercise your right to withdraw or ask any question about how your voice data is managed, please contact:

* **Project Initiative**: Bhasha Setu Tribal Language Data Governance Team
* **Email Contact**: `datagovernance@bhashasetu.org`
* **Local Community Coordinator**: Field Language Liaison Office, Mayurbhanj / Dumka Regional Centers
